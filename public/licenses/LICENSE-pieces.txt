Chess piece SVGs in public/pieces/
==================================

The 12 chess piece SVG files in public/pieces/ are a MODIFIED version of
the Staunty set from Lichess. The local filenames map Lichess's piece
names to Gambit's light/dark naming convention.

One change was made to the upstream SVGs:

  Outline strokes were recolored from neutral to warm dark:
  #3c3c3c -> #4b4847 (light pieces), #1e1e1e -> #312e2b (dark pieces).
  The warmer outline sits better against the board's cream squares.

Nothing else differs. Stroke widths, fills, shading, highlights, path
data, and the 50x50 viewBox are byte-for-byte upstream; reversing the two
color substitutions above reproduces the original files exactly.
